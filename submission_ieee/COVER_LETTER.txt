COVER LETTER - FINAL AUTHOR REVIEW; DATA-USE CLEARANCE STILL UNVERIFIED

Dear Editor,

Please consider "Regional Complementarity and Learned Weighting for Occluded Face Verification" as a regular paper for IEEE Transactions on Biometrics, Behavior, and Identity Science.

The manuscript studies when whole-face and periocular representations complement one another under masks, sunglasses, and neutral conditions. Five configurations were trained on CPU with three seeds each. Same-branch weighting controls distinguish representation learning from the inference fusion rule, and validation-calibrated operating points reveal threshold-transfer limitations. The manuscript reports negative gating findings, exploratory cohort sensitivity, and separate aligned and shared-crop SFace diagnostics. It does not claim a first combination of DINOv2, LoRA, and periocular fusion or universal superiority.

The artifact records frozen protocols, raw scores, exclusion ledgers, and source/checkpoint hashes. Source face images and checkpoint binaries are not redistributed. The ten-page manuscript is accompanied by a separate supplement containing the inference schematic, parameter accounting, preprocessing controls, and provenance records.

The release contains code, manifests, hashes, derived scores, and manuscript figures; source face images and trained checkpoint binaries are excluded. No ethics committee reviewed this project, and no formal approval or exemption is claimed. The study reused existing ROF, MFR2, and RMFRD datasets downloaded from public repositories. The reviewed records do not establish individual participant consent or complete image-use permissions. The applicable ethics-review requirement remains unverified.

The author confirms sole authorship, that the work has not been formally published in a journal or conference, and that it is not currently under review elsewhere. The project GitHub repository includes research drafts and experimental artifacts: https://github.com/sangiya/periocular-fusion-occlusion-robust-face-verification.
