\section{Interpretation and calibration}
Periocular LoRA leads masked AUC; fixed fusion leads sunglasses and exploratory RMFRD; learned gating leads neutral and retained MFR2. Same-branch equal and validation-mean weighting can match or exceed learned gating on ROF. The auxiliary vector is constant, so quality-aware reliability is not established. ROC-constrained TAR is retrospective. Separate thresholds are calibrated on validation identities, with actual test FAR/TAR and acceptance counts saved in every evaluation report. External thresholds transfer from ROF masked validation; external labels do not calibrate them. Masked test FAR resolution is $1/376$, precluding a population security claim at $10^{-3}$.

\section{Reproduction and limitations}
The release ships 120 raw-score CSVs, fifteen evaluation reports, completion records, ten paired AUC contrasts, frozen manifests, source signatures, gate controls, and image ledgers. Exact executed sources are preserved under reproduction/. Checkpoint and pretrained weight binaries, image data, and embedding caches are excluded. Score-level verification recomputes metrics, pair alignment, thresholds, counts, seed summaries, and table values without those binaries. Historical training and post-hoc score filters are not new clean-trained results. The diagnostic SFace baseline has higher AUC with unmatched pretraining/alignment. Author scientific review and any required licensed similarity screening remain separate from arithmetic verification. No journal-readiness or detector-clearance claim is made.
